Physics model
Engine: Ie·dω/dt = Teng(rpm, throttle) − Tclutch. Torque follows a 650 Nm curve minus friction and pumping loss (engine braking); idle control holds ~900 rpm.
Clutch: Tclutch = Tcap(pedal)·tanh(slip/6), capacity up to 900 Nm with a bite point near 60% pedal travel.
Wheels: F = Tclutch·gear·final/r; m·dv/dt = F − rolling(0.015·m·g) − ½ρCdAv² − brake. Stall below 420 rpm.
Real M4 values used: 6MT ratios, 3.15 final drive, ~1.7 t, 650 Nm. Simplified: torque-curve shape, engine inertia, clutch capacity, idle control, tyre grip, steering model.
